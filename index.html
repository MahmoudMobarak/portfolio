Competitions & activities

Here's a collection of extracurricular activities I've participated in to enhance my skills, leadership, and teamwork abilities outside the classroom.

Robotics Program - FLL

Where code meets creation — building robots that think.

As an active member of the school's First LEGO League (FLL) robotics team, I spend the season designing, building, and programming autonomous LEGO robots. Beyond the robot itself, I work on the innovation project side — researching real-world problems and pitching creative solutions to judges. The program has taught me how engineering, coding, and teamwork come together under real competition pressure.

  • Programmed autonomous robots using sensor-based navigation and mission strategy.
  • Collaborated with teammates to brainstorm, prototype, and test robot designs across the season.
  • Presented our innovation project and robot design directly to FLL judges.
  • Learned to stay calm and iterate fast when missions fail mid-competition.

Physics Exhibition

Turning abstract equations into experiments people can touch.

At the school Physics Exhibition, I designed and presented working scientific models that demonstrate core physical principles — turning textbook theory into something visitors can see, touch, and question. Explaining complex ideas to audiences of every age sharpened my ability to communicate science clearly and confidently.

  • Built interactive demonstration models explaining core physics principles.
  • Presented to hundreds of visiting students and faculty across the exhibition.
  • Answered spontaneous deep-dive questions from teachers and judges.
  • Translated abstract equations into visual, hands-on experiences.

Organic Expo

Innovation with a conscience — engineering for a greener future.

Representing sustainable thinking at the Organic Expo, I presented eco-friendly innovations and organic solutions to industry visitors and judges. The expo pushed me to think about how engineering choices affect the environment, and how small design decisions can make products dramatically more sustainable.

  • Presented sustainable innovations to industry visitors and expo judges.
  • Researched the eco-friendly materials and processes behind our showcased solutions.
  • Discussed real-world sustainability trade-offs with professionals.
  • Represented the school at a public-facing environmental event.

Globex Competition

From a homemade filter to a judged engineering stage.

At the Globex competition, I presented a homemade water filter built from layered natural and household materials, demonstrating how basic engineering design can tackle real water-purification problems. The project earned 2nd place and taught me how to take an idea from rough prototype to a judged, professional presentation.
